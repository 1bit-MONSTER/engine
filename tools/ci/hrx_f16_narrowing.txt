# engine#1962: the .loom bodies that narrow an f32 value to f16 (scalar or vector form), at llama.cpp pin 2b7960bae.
# Paths are relative to third_party/llama.cpp/ggml/src/ggml-hrx/kernel-corpus/kernels. This is a
# RATCHET, not a list of bugs: a body here may narrow a bounded value (a scale, a weight) and be
# perfectly correct. What the list buys is that a NEW narrowing body cannot appear without someone
# writing it down and saying what feeds it -- and that is the open question on engine#1962: which of
# these are protected by a producer-side prescale cannot be answered by reading them.
#
# engine#2138 (pin 2f7f04f0d..6485e5d69, llama.cpp #327's AMD merge + #345): 34 bodies added, 1 dropped, 1 recount.
# What feeds each new body is the producer of its fptrunc operand. A script classified them; I checked the
# activation and projection classes against matmul_id/skinny_input_f32_dequant_accumulate_f32,
# matmul/tiled_input_f32_dequant_accumulate_f32 and matmul_id/f16_projection_q4k (the rest share their shape):
#   f32 ACTIVATION narrowed for an f16 WMMA tile, no guard marker (engine#1962's open question; nothing upstream of
#   these is known to prescale): matmul/tiled_input_f32_{,codebook_}dequant_accumulate_f32{,_token64},
#   matmul/tiled_pair_input_f32_dequant_accumulate_f32, matmul/vector_q6_f32_body, matmul_id/{skinny,tiled,pair}_input_
#   f32_dequant_accumulate_f32, matmul_id/f16_pair_accumulate, copy/convert_f32_f16.
#   ROUTED f16 projections, AMD's composable copies (matmul_id/f16_projection_q{4,5,6}k): the dequantized value and the
#   loaded activation are narrowed with NO guard marker, where this line's mul_mat_id_q{4,5,6}k_f16_wmma_projection carry
#   one (hrx_f16_guards.txt, engine#2127). They are library sources of the dispatched
#   ggml_mul_mat_id_swiglu_f32_f32_wmma{,_iq3s_table}. Whether those kernels reach these functions is not established.
#   DEQUANTIZED WEIGHTS (bounded by the format's scale): the dequant callers in the matmul motifs above, the
#   rmsnorm-weight multiplies in gated_delta_net/rmsnorm_gate and ops/rows/get_rows_rmsnorm_binary_q8_1_x4_f16;
#   dequant.loom 20 -> 21 is #345's ggml_codebook_f16_vector4.
#   ATTENTION probabilities and softmax scales (in [0,1] or a 1/sum): flash_attention/{online_softmax,decode_partials,
#   qk_block_wmma,pv_block_wmma,publish_f32}.
#   PUBLISHED OUTPUTS stored as f16 (a result's own range; a KV cache or a normalized row): rows/cache_publish,
